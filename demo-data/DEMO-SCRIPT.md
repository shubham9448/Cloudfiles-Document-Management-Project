# Copperline demo: script and run of show

**Written for:** you, to narrate to the competition judges. Target length: about 4 minutes after editing.
**Deal used:** OPP-3001 Wheel loader purchase (Redwood Ridge Landscaping, Elena Marquez). Rehearse on OPP-3002 first.

## The story in one line

"Copperline lent against an expired driving licence twice last quarter, because nobody checked the date. Now the system reads the licence, flags the problem, blocks the deal, and clears itself when a renewed licence arrives. When the deal closes, it writes the welcome letter and shares the paperwork with the supplier safely."

Three products, one story: **Document AI** reads, **Document Generation** writes, **Document Management** files and shares.

## Before you press record

1. Import the records (see `README.md`) and wait for the folders to appear.
2. Upload deal 1's invoice, application form, signed agreement and delivery photo **into the deal folder itself**. Wait until the invoice fields fill and the files sit in their subfolders. Then you can start the video with documents already sorted, or record this part live and cut the waiting.
3. Make sure the Driving Licence section is **empty** and the stage is **Prospecting**.
4. Set **Supplier Contact Email** to an address you control. Open that inbox in another tab.
5. Have `drivers-license-EXPIRED.pdf`, `utility-bill.pdf` and `drivers-license-VALID.pdf` ready in one folder.
6. Close other tabs and notifications. Set the browser zoom to about 110%.
7. Rehearse the whole thing on OPP-3002 once, with its VALID licence, so you know the waits.

## What to say and what not to say

- **Do say** that Document AI "confirms it is a driving licence". I tested that against the specimen licences.
- **Don't say** the link is "restricted to the supplier's email" or "needs verification" unless you've opened the link in an Incognito window and seen the prompt. I haven't been able to confirm it.
- **Don't promise** the link "expires". The link settings I used have no expiry. Say it "can be switched off at any time".
- **Share with Supplier currently shares whole folders.** The Agreement folder includes the generated draft agreement and the welcome letter, which the supplier shouldn't get. In the demo, **tick only the application form and the supplier invoice**, and say "identity documents are never offered". I haven't fixed this yet, so don't tick the agreement box.
- The email may be slow or land in spam. If it doesn't arrive in time, show the email in the deal's **Activity** timeline instead, then cut to it arriving later.

## If something goes wrong mid-take

- Fields don't fill after a minute: stop, check the CloudFiles Events tab, and re-record the clip. Don't try to fix it on camera.
- Wrong folder: delete the file, wait for the delete event, and upload again to the **deal folder**, not a subfolder.
- Use the second deal (OPP-3002) as a spare. Don't reuse a deal you've already closed.

## Editing tips

- Record in short clips, one per row of the table, and join them. Don't try one perfect take.
- Speed up the waits (the licence read and the letter) to about 4x, with a small "about 60 seconds" caption.
- Put the product names on screen as they appear: Document AI, Document Generation, Document Management.
- Keep the final video under five minutes.
